# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Copilot Metrics Dashboard to be a welcoming, professional, and privacy-conscious OSS project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat each other with respect across issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect private data, credentials, and customer context.
- Credit ideas and contributions appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, customer data, or sensitive security details in public channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainers through the most private GitHub channel available for the repository. For security-sensitive or privacy-sensitive reports, follow `SECURITY.md` and avoid public details.
